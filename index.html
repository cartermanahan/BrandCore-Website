Do I need any design experience?

No. You describe the idea in plain words. BrandFounder generates a direction you can review and refine using the options available in the app.

What actually lands in my library?

A logo direction with vector export, a palette, typography choices, launch visuals, a reusable brand backdrop, AI-assisted market and pricing starting points, positioning, promotion guidance and a 30-day launch schedule. Review name, trademark and originality questions before commercial use; the app shows the current pack contents before purchase.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. The onboarding generates your logo and palette as a preview before you make an account. You can't export or copy anything at that stage — it's there so you can judge the direction before deciding.

Can I change what it makes?

Yes. Regenerate the mark, swap tones, rework the wordmark, or push the direction somewhere else. Nothing is locked.

How long does the whole thing take?

Brand generation normally takes minutes, but timing varies with request complexity, connectivity and provider availability. The 30-day plan then paces the launch out for you, day by day.

Will it work for my kind of business?

If you can describe what you'd sell and who'd buy it, it has enough to work with — physical products, digital ones, or services.

Is it only on iPhone?

iPhone for now, with macOS coming. The iPhone app is the currently available version.

What does the full pack cost?

The UK Brand Pack is a £17.99 one-time in-app purchase. Regional App Store pricing may vary, and the price shown by Apple before purchase is the price that applies.

Who handles billing and refunds?

Apple processes App Store billing and refund requests under its policies; your statutory consumer rights are unaffected. Contact BrandFounder support if a generation or delivery problem needs investigating.

How are analytics and generation inputs handled?

Optional product analytics is off unless you enable it. Inputs needed for an AI feature are still processed when you request that feature after the in-app disclosure. “Share inputs to improve results” is a separate setting and is also off by default. See the Privacy Policy for details.
